## 2024-05-18 - [Fix Buffer Overflow]\n**Vulnerability:** Fixed buffer overflows in sprintf calls when reading from GetTmpDir or getenv('TMP').\n**Learning:** In wrappedlibc.c, some stack buffers for paths were fixed at size 100 which might cause buffer overflows when standard getenv/tmp directories are longer.\n**Prevention:** Replace sprintf with snprintf and increase sizes to MAX_PATH.
